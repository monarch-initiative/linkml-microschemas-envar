    Conditionally core Required in specific contexts 1 field
    H3 Resolutionconditionally corerequired

    h3_resolution · range Integer · cardinality 1

    Honeycomb maps come in 16 standard sizes. This number says which one: 0 is continent-sized cells, 15 is about a square metre.

    H3 resolution of the hexagons, 0 (coarsest) to 15 (finest). Cell area and edge length follow from it through H3's own tables.

    Example

    3 — CHEL 2024

    Why it matters & mappings

    H3 resolutions in use differ by orders of magnitude in cell area, so "H3" alone does not say how coarse a value is.

    See also: https://h3geo.org/docs/core-library/restable/
